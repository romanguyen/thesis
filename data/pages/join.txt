====== Get Account in VO ======
Join MetaCentrum Virtual Organization to access computing and storage resources.

===== Who can become a member? =====
MetaCentrum services are available free of charge to members of Czech academic and research institutions. Students, researchers, and staff at universities and research institutes are eligible to join.

===== How to join =====
  * 1. Get an identity - You need an identity from eduID.cz federation (e.g., your university login) or get a guest identity from Hostel eduID.cz.
  * 2. Register to VO - Fill in the registration form to join MetaCentrum Virtual Organization through the Perun system.
  * 3. Wait for approval - Your registration will be processed, usually within one working day. You will receive an email confirmation.

===== Ready to join? =====

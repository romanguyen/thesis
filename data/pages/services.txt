====== Services ======
Overview of services provided by MetaCentrum NGI for the academic and research community.

===== Computing Services =====
MetaCentrum provides access to high-performance computing resources for researchers across Czech Republic. Our infrastructure includes clusters, grids, and specialized computing platforms.

  * Batch job processing
  * Interactive computing
  * GPU computing
  * Parallel processing

===== MetaCloud =====
MetaCloud is our Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS) cloud platform, providing virtual machines and flexible computing resources on demand.

[[services:cloud|Learn more about MetaCloud]]

===== Data Storage =====
We provide various storage solutions including home directories, scratch storage for computation, and long-term archival storage for research data.

  * Home directories
  * Scratch storage
  * Archival storage
  * Data sharing

===== Certificates =====
Digital certificates are required for accessing grid resources and services. MetaCentrum supports certificates issued by CESNET CA and other trusted authorities.

[[https://pki.cesnet.cz|Get a certificate from CESNET PKI]]

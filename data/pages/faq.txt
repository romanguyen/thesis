===== Frequently Asked Questions =====

==== How do I get an account? ====
You need to register through the Perun system using your eduID.cz identity. See the [[join|Get Account]] page for details.

==== What resources are available? ====
MetaCentrum provides access to computing clusters, cloud resources, and storage. See the [[resources|Resources]] page for a complete list.

==== How do I submit jobs? ====
Jobs are submitted using the PBS scheduling system. Documentation is available in our user guides.

==== Who can use MetaCentrum? ====
Members of Czech academic and research institutions can use MetaCentrum services free of charge.
